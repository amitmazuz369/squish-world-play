Squish World Greybox

Five gates. Three creatures. Every creature you pick up carries one way of moving, and that is the only thing that opens anything.

Nothing tells you which gate needs what. Walk into a gate you can't pass and the world remembers it for you — a shape appears above it. You'll learn what the shape means when you meet the creature that matches it.
